AstraPromos

Promotional Video & Affiliate Content Management Platform

AstraPromos is a web dashboard designed for content creators and affiliate marketers to manage product deals, preview promotional short videos, configure captions and privacy settings, and publish directly to their connected TikTok accounts.

Core Capabilities:
  • Secure TikTok OAuth2 authentication via Login Kit
  • Video preview, caption editing, and privacy selection (Public, Friends, Only Me)
  • Direct publishing to TikTok via Content Posting API
