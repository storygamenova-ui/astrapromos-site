Privacy Policy — AstraPromos

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit, AstraPromos receives only the authorized basic profile information (open_id, display name, avatar URL) and OAuth access/refresh tokens required to publish videos on your behalf.

2. How We Use Information

Your credentials and tokens are used exclusively to display your connected creator profile in the dashboard and upload or publish promotional videos that you explicitly approve.

3. Data Sharing & Storage

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or TikTok tokens with third parties. OAuth tokens are stored securely and used solely for authorized API requests.

4. Data Deletion & Contact

You may revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings or request deletion of stored data by emailing storygamenova@gmail.com.
